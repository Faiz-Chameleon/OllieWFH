## Games tab: whole screen slid up when swiped (local, not shipped)

- **Symptom (still there after the 2026-10-05 Tic Tac Toe fix):** on the Games tab, a swipe moved the whole screen up and left empty space.
- **Cause:** the Games tab itself (`lib/CareCircle/assistance/games_selection_screen.dart`) is a scrollable `GridView` of four tiles. Swipe tests measured the tiles moving **~200px on iOS** (bounce) and ~13px on Android (stretch). Yesterday's fix only covered the Tic Tac Toe board inside one game, which still holds. Pac-Man, Tetris and 2048 were measured and don't move.
- **Fix:** tiles are sized in a `LayoutBuilder` to fit between the app bar and HomeMain's floating bottom bar (8% bar + 6% padding, so 0.15 of the screen height is reserved), keeping the original 0.9 aspect ratio when it fits and shrinking on short screens. The grid uses `NeverScrollableScrollPhysics`. The diff is limited to the layout (no reformatting).
- **Test:** `test/games_screen_layout_test.dart` covers 4 phone sizes × Android/iOS. It checks that every tile label sits above the bottom bar and that nothing moves during or after a swipe. 8/8 pass; the same test fails 8/8 on the old screen. Full app suite: 59/59.
- **Ships with** the next app build.
